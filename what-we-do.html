Our Design Capability

Advanced Digital & Structural Excellence

Façade design is not merely detailing—it is the synthesis of structural wind loads, seismic movements, thermal expansion, fixing metallurgy, and mould economy.

BIM & 3D Computational Modelling

Our in-house design studio utilizes Rhino 3D, Grasshopper, Autodesk Revit, SketchUp, and Navisworks to coordinate complex double-curved surfaces, parametric louvers, and panel joints directly with main consultants and architects.

Structural Optimization & Engineering Calculations

Full structural engineering calculations for wind load pressure, dead weight, framing sub-structure, anchor brackets, thermal expansion gaps, and seismic drift allowances in full compliance with UAE Fire & Life Safety Codes.

Value Engineering & Panel Rationalization

By standardizing mould repetition while preserving organic external aesthetic contours, we reduce project lifecycle costs and drastically accelerate manufacturing lead times.
