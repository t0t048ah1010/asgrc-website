01 / Engineering & Architecture

Translating Bold Architectural Intent into
Buildable Systems

From early concept geometry and BIM integration to FEA structural validation, ASGRC provides full design-assist to optimize form, weight, and panelization.

BIM & 3D Computational Modelling

Our in-house design studio utilizes Rhino 3D, Grasshopper, Autodesk Revit, SketchUp, and Navisworks to coordinate complex double-curved surfaces, parametric louvers, and panel joints directly with main consultants and architects.

Structural Optimization & Engineering

Full structural engineering calculations for wind load pressure, dead weight, framing sub-structure, anchor brackets, thermal expansion gaps, and seismic drift allowances — fully compliant with UAE Fire & Life Safety Codes.

Value Engineering & Panel Rationalization

By standardizing mould repetition while preserving organic external aesthetic contours, we reduce project lifecycle costs and drastically accelerate manufacturing lead times.

Design Workflow

From Concept to Construction-Ready

01

Concept & Geometry Capture

02

BIM & Structural Analysis

03

Value Engineering

Optimize panel sizes, joint layouts, and mould strategies to reduce cost and lead time.

04

Shop Drawings & Approval

Deliver production-ready shop drawings, anchor calculations, and fixing details for consultant sign-off.
