Primary Façade Cladding

GFRC / GRC

Glass Fiber Reinforced Concrete

Composed of high-grade white or grey Portland cement, silica sand, polymers, and alkali-resistant (AR) glass fibers. GFRC provides extreme strength with only 18–20 mm skin thickness, reducing building dead-load by up to 75% compared to traditional precast concrete.

Density: 1,800 – 2,100 kg/m³
Fire Rating: Class A1 Non-Combustible (BS 8414 & EN 13501-1)
Applications: Façades, spandrels, curvilinear fins, sunscreens
Ultra-High Strength Matrix

UHPC

Ultra High Performance Concrete

An advanced cementitious composite with compressive strengths exceeding 120–150 MPa and exceptional flexural ductility. Allows ultra-thin panels (12–15 mm) with nearly zero porosity, impervious to chloride ingress and salt spray.

Compressive Strength: > 130 MPa
Water Absorption: < 1.5%
Applications: Slim louvers, perforated mashrabiya screens, high-wear cladding
