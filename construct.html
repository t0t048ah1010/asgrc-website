Installation Process

From Survey to Handover

01

3D Laser Scanning

Total station and laser point cloud surveys of structural slabs and steel columns to absorb site tolerances prior to bracket installation.

02

Precision Framing & Lifting

03

Acoustic barriers, non-combustible insulation, certified fire barrier cavities, silicone joint weatherproofing, and final handover.
