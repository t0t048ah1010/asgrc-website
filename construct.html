Flawless On-Site Execution

Even the most precise factory panels depend on immaculate on-site craneage, sub-framing alignment, and weather sealing across extreme high-rise environments.

3D Laser Scanning & Digital Setting-Out

Our site engineers perform detailed 3D total station and laser point cloud surveys of structural slabs and steel columns to absorb site tolerances prior to bracket installation.

Precision Framing & Engineered Lifting

High-accuracy hoisting using dedicated tower crane rigs, spider cranes, and monorail winches for large-format panel positioning without micro-fractures.

Waterproofing, Fire Stopping & Handover

Complete installation of acoustic barriers, non-combustible rockwool insulation, certified Dubai & Abu Dhabi Civil Defense fire barrier cavities, silicone joint weatherproofing, and final digital handover.
