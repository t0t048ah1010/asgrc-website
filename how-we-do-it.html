Precision Manufacturing
with CNC Technology

Fully automatic CNC mould engineering. A1-quality production of GRC, GRP, GRG, and UHPC systems with superior process control, custom surface engineering, textures, finishes, and end-to-end quality assurance.

CNC Mould Engineering

Fully automatic CNC mould engineering for complex geometries. Precision-milled aluminium and GRP moulds capable of producing repeatable panels with tolerances of ±0.5mm.

Quality Assurance

Superior process control with ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management. Every panel undergoes dimensional inspection, visual assessment, and structural verification before dispatch.

Custom Finishes & Textures

Surface engineering with custom textures, pigments, and coatings. From polished stone finishes to rustic stone-effect textures — each panel can be tailored to the architect's vision.
