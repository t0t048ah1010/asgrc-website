Our History

Two Decades of Crafting Landmarks

Al Shafar GRC LLC (ASGRC) commenced operations in 2003 with an initial production capacity of 100 m² per day. Over the last twenty-plus years, relentless technical innovation, capital investment in robotics, and deep engineering partnerships have propelled ASGRC to exceed 1,000 m² per day across its flagship plant in Dubai Industrial City (DIC).

Operating as part of Al Shafar Group, ASGRC provides an unmatched integrated delivery model: combining early design engineering, mould fabrication, composite manufacturing, and turnkey on-site façade construction.

Sustainability Commitment

Building Responsibly for a Green UAE

ASGRC is committed to minimizing environmental impact across all stages of production. Our low-embodied-carbon GFRC mix designs, closed-loop water filtration, 100% recyclable GRP mould patterns, and localized aggregate sourcing support Dubai Clean Energy Strategy and UAE Net Zero commitments.
