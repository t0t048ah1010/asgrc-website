Who We Are

ASGRC is a leading manufacturer of GRC, GRP and GRG architectural systems engineered for façades, interior features and complex structural elements. Our solutions combine design aesthetics with precision, durability and long‑term performance, supporting architects, designers and developers across the UAE.

Our Expertise

GRC Manufacturing

Advanced spray and premix GRC systems engineered for façades, cladding, screens, cornices, domes and architectural detailing.

GRP Fabrication

High‑strength GRP moulding for curved structures, canopies, domes and lightweight architectural features requiring flexibility and precision.

GRG Interior Works

Smooth, fire‑resistant GRG systems for ceilings, wall panels, columns and luxury interior environments.

Our Mission

To deliver sustainable and expressive composite solutions that elevate architectural design while ensuring long‑term performance and reliability.

Our Vision

To be recognized as the most trusted provider of GRC, GRP and GRG systems, known for quality, innovation and architectural excellence.
