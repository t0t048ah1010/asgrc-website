Our Story

From Local Pioneers to Regional Leaders

Founded in 2004, ASGRC began as a pioneering GRC manufacturer in the UAE, serving the region's rapid construction boom with innovative façade solutions.

Today, we operate a state-of-the-art manufacturing facility, employ over 200 skilled professionals, and deliver turnkey façade packages to landmark projects across 15+ countries.

Our integrated approach — spanning design engineering, precision manufacturing, and turnkey installation — makes us uniquely positioned to deliver complex façade systems with confidence.
