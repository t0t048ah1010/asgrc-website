Our Process

Design → Manufacture → Construct

Façade Engineering & Design

BIM modelling, structural validation, and value engineering — optimizing geometry for GFRC or UHPC execution with full consultant coordination.

02

Precision Manufacturing

Spray & projection processes in our climate-controlled UAE facility. CNC-milled steel moulds, robotic vibratory compaction, and steam curing.

3D laser setting-out, engineered lifting, structural bracket installation, waterproofing, Civil Defense compliance, and full handover.
