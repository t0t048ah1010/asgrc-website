Advanced Materials & Systems

Engineered for strength, durability, and design flexibility.

Each component is optimized for structural efficiency, weather resistance, and long-term performance through controlled manufacturing and rigorous testing.

GFRC / GRC

Glass Fiber Reinforced Concrete—a thin concrete shell with alkali-resistant glass fibers, engineered for complex, sculptural geometry in sections as thin as 1–1½ in.

  • Enables complex, sculptural geometry with thin sections
  • Lightweight yet strong, ideal for expressive façades
  • Excellent durability—resists corrosion, UV, and freeze–thaw
  • Creative form freedom with efficient installation

UHPC

Ultra High Performance Concrete—ultra-thin, high-strength panels (½–¾ in) with dense material matrix for smooth, precise surfaces and tight dimensional tolerances.

  • Ultra-thin, high-strength panels with structural-grade performance
  • Dense material matrix for smooth, precise surfaces
  • Exceptional durability—resists freeze–thaw, abrasion, chloride
  • Advanced material system delivering longevity and refinement
