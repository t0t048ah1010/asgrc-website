Our Materials

Advanced Materials &
Advanced Systems

Our façade systems leverage advanced cementitious materials — UHPC and GFRC — engineered for strength, durability, and design flexibility.

GRC

Glass Fiber Reinforced Concrete

A thin concrete shell of cement, sand, and alkali-resistant glass fibers supported by a secondary steel frame.

  • Complex, sculptural geometry with thin sections (1–1½ in)
  • Lightweight yet strong, ideal for expressive façade applications
  • Excellent durability — resists corrosion, UV, and freeze–thaw cycles
UHPC

Ultra High Performance Concrete

A thin-walled cladding of high-performance cement, fine aggregates, and steel or synthetic fibers with exceptionally high strength.

  • Ultra-thin, high-strength panels (½–¾ in) with structural-grade performance
  • Dense material matrix for smooth, precise surfaces and tight tolerances
  • Exceptional durability — resists freeze–thaw, abrasion, and chloride penetration
GRP

Glass Fiber Reinforced Plastic

Composite panels from 3mm to 8mm thick with non-combustible layer configurations for versatile architectural applications.

  • GRP thickness: 3mm – 8mm with optimized layer configuration
  • Non-combustible deck options and fire-retardant performance
  • Suitable for interiors, exteriors, roofs, and decorative screens
GRG

Glass Fiber Reinforced Gypsum

Interior decorative elements, cornices, columns, and intricate gypsum-based architectural features.

  • Intricate decorative elements for luxury interiors
  • Cornices, columns, medallions, and custom moldings
  • Smooth finish ready for painting and detailing
