Why We Do It

Superior solutions to drive superior outcomes.

Our façade systems leverage advanced cementitious materials—GRC and UHPC—engineered for strength, durability, and design flexibility. Each component is optimized for structural efficiency, weather resistance, and long-term performance through controlled DIC manufacturing.

GFRC System

Glass Fiber Reinforced Concrete

Thin concrete shell of cement, silica sand, and alkali-resistant glass fibers supported by a secondary steel frame for large-format architectural envelopes.

  • ✓ Enables complex sculptural geometry with thin sections (18–20 mm)
  • ✓ Lightweight yet high-strength, ideal for high-rise envelopes
  • ✓ Extreme durability — resists UAE heat, UV, and marine humidity

UHPC System

Ultra High Performance Concrete

Thin-walled cladding made of high-performance cement, fine aggregates, and specialized fibers with exceptional compressive and flexural strength.

  • ✓ Ultra-thin, high-strength panels (12–15 mm) with structural rigidity
  • ✓ Dense material matrix for ultra-smooth surface and tight tolerances
  • ✓ Superior resistance to chloride penetration, abrasion, and weathering
