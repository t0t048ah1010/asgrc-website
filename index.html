Materials

Engineered systems.
Limitless forms.

Four core material systems, each engineered for performance, aesthetics, and durability.

Core material

GFRC

Glass-fibre reinforced concrete. Lightweight, high-strength panels for cladding, cornices, and ornamental elements. Ideal for complex geometries and curved façades.

6–12 mm Panel thickness
45–55 MPa Compressive strength
Advanced

UHPC

Ultra-high-performance concrete. Exceptional durability and minimal section depth. Used for high-rise cladding, architectural screens, and precision-cut façades.

8–20 mm Panel thickness
150+ MPa Compressive strength
Lightweight

GRP

Glass-reinforced plastic. Formable into complex organic shapes. Perfect for sculptural façades, canopies, and decorative screens with weather-resistant finishes.

3–8 mm Panel thickness
Low weight Specific gravity
Interior

GRG

Glass-reinforced gypsum. Interior ceilings, cornices, and decorative elements. Seamless joints, paint-ready finish, rapid installation.

4–10 mm Panel thickness
