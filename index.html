As part of the prestigious Al Shafar Group of Companies, ASGRC is one of the UAE's premier manufacturers of bespoke glass fiber reinforced products. Operating from a state-of-the-art manufacturing facility in Dubai Industrial City (DIC)—the largest of its kind in the region—we deliver complete, high-end engineered solutions from design to manufacturing, site logistics, and final installation. Backed by over 20 years of expert craftsmanship, we specialize in delivering robust GFRC, GFRG, GFRP, and UHPC elements engineered to support the region's most iconic and complex architectural landmarks.
