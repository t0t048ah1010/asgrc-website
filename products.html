Our Materials

Advanced Materials &
Advanced Systems

Our façade systems leverage advanced cementitious materials — UHPC and GFRC — engineered for strength, durability, and design flexibility. Each material system is optimized for specific applications across residential, commercial, hospitality, and institutional sectors.

GRC

Glass Fiber Reinforced Concrete

A thin concrete shell of cement, sand, and alkali-resistant glass fibers supported by a secondary steel frame. Engineered for complex geometries and expressive architectural applications.

Key Properties

  • Thickness: 1–1½ inches with secondary steel frame support
  • Compressive strength: >6,000 PSI at 28 days
  • Excellent durability — resists corrosion, UV, and freeze–thaw cycles
  • Ideal for sculptural, parametric, and organic façade designs
Request GRC Spec Sheet
UHPC

Ultra High Performance Concrete

A thin-walled cladding of high-performance cement, fine aggregates, and steel or synthetic fibers with exceptionally high strength and dense material matrix.

Key Properties

  • Thickness: ½–¾ inches with structural-grade performance
  • Compressive strength: >15,000 PSI at 28 days
  • Dense material matrix — smooth, precise surfaces with tight tolerances
  • Exceptional durability — resists freeze–thaw, abrasion, and chloride penetration
Request UHPC Spec Sheet
GRP

Glass Fiber Reinforced Plastic

Composite panels from 3mm to 8mm thick with non-combustible layer configurations for versatile architectural applications across interiors and exteriors.

Key Properties

  • GRP thickness: 3mm – 8mm with optimized layer configuration
  • Non-combustible deck options and fire-retardant performance
  • Suitable for interiors, exteriors, roofs, and decorative screens
  • Lightweight with high strength-to-weight ratio
Request GRP Spec Sheet
GRG

Glass Fiber Reinforced Gypsum

Interior decorative elements, cornices, columns, and intricate gypsum-based architectural features for luxury residential and hospitality projects.

Key Properties

  • Intricate decorative elements for luxury interiors
  • Cornices, columns, medallions, and custom moldings
  • Smooth finish ready for painting and detailing
  • Lightweight with excellent acoustic properties
