Our Products

ASGRC manufactures high‑performance composite materials including GRC, GRP and GRG, engineered for architectural façades, interior features and complex structural designs.

GRC

Glass Reinforced Concrete (GRC) is a lightweight, durable material ideal for architectural façades, cladding, screens, cornices, domes and decorative elements.

ASGRC produces GRC using advanced spray and premix techniques, ensuring high strength, weather resistance and long‑term performance.

GRP

Glass Reinforced Plastic (GRP) is a high‑strength composite used for domes, canopies, complex curved structures, moulded shapes and lightweight architectural features.

GRP offers excellent flexibility, corrosion resistance and design freedom, making it ideal for custom architectural applications.

GRG

Glass Reinforced Gypsum (GRG) is designed for interior applications such as ceilings, wall panels, cornices, columns and decorative interior features.

GRG provides smooth finishes, precise detailing and fire‑resistant performance, making it ideal for luxury interior environments.
